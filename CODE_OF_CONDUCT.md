# Code of Conduct

ModelCairn contributors are expected to communicate professionally, welcome
good-faith questions, criticize ideas rather than people, and avoid harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, or publication of private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the
repository's [private security reporting channel](https://github.com/sxamx/modelcairn/security/advisories/new) or GitHub moderation tools. Enforcement decisions
should be proportional, documented privately when appropriate, and avoid exposing
reporters unnecessarily.
